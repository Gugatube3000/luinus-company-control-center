# Austrian economics reading list

_Ordered path, not a pile. Everything free at mises.org/library._

## Tier 1 — before Mises University (the sprint)

1. Henry Hazlitt — *Economics in One Lesson*
2. Murray Rothbard — *What Has Government Done to Our Money?*
3. Ludwig von Mises — *Economic Policy: Thoughts for Today and Tomorrow*
4. Robert Murphy — *Choice: Cooperation, Enterprise, and Human Action*

## Tier 2 — the core (fall, alongside classes)

5. Ludwig von Mises — *Human Action* (the scholar's edition; slow is fine)
6. Murray Rothbard — *Man, Economy, and State*
7. Carl Menger — *Principles of Economics* (where it all started, 1871)
8. F.A. Hayek — "The Use of Knowledge in Society" (essay) + *Prices and Production*

## Tier 3 — depth & modern voices

9. Eugen von Böhm-Bawerk — *Capital and Interest* (selections)
10. Israel Kirzner — *Competition and Entrepreneurship*
11. Jesús Huerta de Soto — *Money, Bank Credit, and Economic Cycles*
12. Per Bylund — *The Seen, the Unseen, and the Unrealized* (entrepreneurship — relevant to Luinus/Fortis)

## Keep sharp

- Mises Wire + *Quarterly Journal of Austrian Economics* for current debates
- *Human Action* podcast-style lectures from past MUs (mises.org media)
- Practice: for every econ class topic this fall, write 3 lines on "what would
  the Austrian critique be?" — best interview differentiator you have
