# Mises University prep — 2 weeks (Jul 5 → Jul 18)

Goal: arrive in Auburn already fluent in the core framework so the week
compounds instead of introduces. MU lectures assume familiarity with the
action-axiom approach, marginal utility, Austrian capital theory, and ABCT.

## The sprint

| Days | Read | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| Jul 5–8 | **Hazlitt, *Economics in One Lesson*** (~200pp, fast) | The reflexes: seen vs. unseen |
| Jul 9–13 | **Rothbard, *What Has Government Done to Our Money?*** + **Mises, *Economic Policy*** (both short) | Money/inflation + Mises's accessible lecture style |
| Jul 14–17 | **Murphy, *Choice*** (guided tour of *Human Action*) — or selected *Human Action* chapters (I: Acting Man; IV: Praxeology basics; XX: Interest, Credit Expansion) | The praxeological core MU is built on |
| Jul 18 | Skim notes; write 5 questions to ask faculty | Go in with questions, leave with answers |

All of these are free PDFs/audiobooks at mises.org/library.
