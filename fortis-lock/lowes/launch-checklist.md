# Lowe's launch checklist — go-live 2026-07-06

_The jump from marketplace to retail. Check every box; a missed compliance
item at a big-box retailer means chargebacks._

## Product & inventory

- [ ] PO quantities confirmed shipped and **received** by Lowe's DC/stores
- [ ] Packaging meets Lowe's spec (UPC/barcode scans, labeling, no damage)
- [ ] Item setup verified in Lowe's system: price, description, images correct

## Systems & compliance

- [ ] LowesLink / Vendor Gateway access working; right people have logins
- [ ] EDI flow tested (POs in, ASNs + invoices out) — no manual gaps
- [ ] Insurance / vendor compliance docs current
- [ ] Chargeback policy read; top 5 chargeback causes listed and mitigated

## Launch (online + in-store)

- [ ] Lowes.com listing live: title, images, features, correct category
- [ ] In-store: planogram placement known; can someone physically verify a store on 07-06?
- [ ] Pricing coherent across Lowe's vs Amazon (no channel conflict surprise)

## Day 1–7 monitoring

- [ ] Daily: POS/sell-through numbers pulled from vendor portal
- [ ] Daily: Lowes.com reviews + Q&A checked
- [ ] Amazon rank/stock watched for retail spillover
- [ ] Week-1 recap written → `../tasks.md` P1 review item

## Relationship

- [ ] Launch-day note to the buyer/merchant (short, data will follow)
- [ ] 30-day check-in scheduled with sell-through story
